Profiles are organizational records, not accounts. Your signed-in account's server-enforced permissions determine access. Switching workspace profiles does not sign in or impersonate anyone. Share GM notes only through explicit ACLs. Never store passwords or authentication secrets in notes.

Campaign management

Imports create independent copies with new IDs; they never overwrite existing data. Exports include referenced profiles and required genre/campaign/world and object-availability context. Full backups of reusable definitions are available on the Shared definition library page.

People

Open Worlds to create worlds and select their parent campaign, ownership, and subscriptions. Create Genres first, then Campaigns. Use Object availability to link one object to multiple genres, campaigns, and worlds or make it global. Links share edits; they are not permissions.

📜 Wurm Tools — Comprehensive Suite Concepts

Wurm Tools is engineered as a clean, modular, client-side dashboard with starter tools built to expand. Below is an overview of the planned suite roadmap documented in CONCEPTS.md:

🏰 Settlement & Deed Operations

  • Deed Upkeep & Size Calculator (Active): Size ($W \times H$), perimeter buffers, guard upkeep, and runway days based on bank silver.
  • Perimeter & Border Planner: Neighbor buffer rules, expansion pacing, and perimeter tile costs.
  • House & Building Material Estimator: Brick, mortar, plank, and roofing requirements for multi-story buildings.
  • Deed Roles & Permissions Matrix: Cheat sheet for Citizen, Ally, Non-citizen, and Enemy permissions.

⏳ Timers, Rituals & Religion

  • Action & Prayer Timers (Active): 20-minute favor cooldown, 30-minute meditation, 3-hour path ability, and custom timers with audio chime and browser notification alerts.
  • Priest Favor & Spell Matrix: Favor regeneration, alignment requirements, and deity spell lists (Fo, Magranon, Vynora, Libila).
  • Sleep Bonus Duration Tracker: Sleep powder duration and bonus tick optimization.

🧭 Navigation, Cartography & Travel

  • Distance & Bearing Calculator (Active): Euclidean tile distance, metric conversion (1 tile = 4 meters), compass bearings, and travel time by foot, mount, cart, or ship.
  • Waypoint Log (Active): Store favorite deed, mine, and waypoint coordinates locally.
  • Slope & Terraforming Helper: Elevation difference and cart slope safety rules.

⚒️ Crafting, Metallurgy & Smithing

  • Alloy Ratios & Smelting Matrix: Exact mass formulas for bronze, brass, steel, and electrum.
  • Item Improvement & Max QL Guide: Skill vs. tool QL caps, repair math, and failure thresholds.
  • God Rune Compendium: Rune bonuses, combinations, and conflict resolution.

🌾 Farming, Agriculture & Cooking

  • Crop & Forestry Starfall Calendar: Optimal planting and harvesting periods across Wurm's seasonal calendar.
  • CCFP Nutrition Balancer: Calorie, Carbohydrate, Fat, and Protein optimization for skill gain bonuses.
  • Animal Breeding & Trait Matrix: Speed, strength, and stamina trait inheritance tracking.

📝 Player Log & Notebook

  • Settlement & Player Notes (Active): Record vein coordinates, chore lists, and crafting milestones with localStorage persistence and JSON export.
